Discussion Session Week 1

William Duan

ECON 101, Fall 2020

Q1

Suppose Kovir and Nilfgard can both produce wheat or swords. Assume that in a year, a Nilfgardian can either produce 10 bushels of wheat, or produce 4 swords. Meanwhile, someone in Kovir can produce 5 bushels of wheat or 5 swords a year. Suppose further that Nilfgard has a population of 4,000,000 while Kovir has a population of 1,000,000. Draw the PPF of Nilfgard and Kovir. Which country has the comparative advantage in swords and which in wheat? Which country has an absolute advantage in wheat?

Q2

After the signing of the First Treaty of Lan Exeter, Kovir and Nilfgard start to trade. What would each country produce? What would each country import and export?

Q3

Now consider Toussaint, a country with 500,000 people. Suppose someone in Toussaint can produce either 4 bushels of wheat or 1 sword a year. Would Nilfgard benefit from trading with Toussaint? Would Toussaint benefit from trading with Nilfgard? Which country would produce swords and which country would produce wheat?
