UBC surveys students at the end of every term, and every report I have received is listed under its course. The overall rating is the answer to the report's summary question, “How would you rate the overall contribution of the TA to the course?”, scored from 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good) and reported by UBC as an interpolated median. The share rating good or very good is taken over the students who answered that question and averaged across a course's reports. Fall is September to December and Winter January to April.
